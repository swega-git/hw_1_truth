I modelled my code directly on my ER diagram, focusing on the relationships between the six entities and their attributes. I used a junction table called Enrollment to represent the many-to-many relationship between students and courses. I also introduced a uniqueness constraint to ensure that each student can enroll in a particular course only once.

The Claude-generated model is substantially more sophisticated than mine, but it is also more complex. It includes additional features such as enrollment status, detailed course scheduling, QR-code expiration, attendance verification, and duplicate-image detection. These features could be useful in a real attendance management system, particularly for preventing duplicate attendance records and reducing opportunities for cheating.
